Our story

A tandoor on Botanic Avenue

Raj and Meena Sharma opened the first Kesar in 2004 with eleven tables and a clay oven shipped from Punjab. Students came for the butter chicken, families came back for Sunday biryani, and twenty years later there are five of us.

Every restaurant still has its own tandoor and makes its sauces from scratch each morning. Head chef Arjun trains every new cook in Belfast before they move to a kitchen of their own.

Questions

Good to know

Anything else? Ring your local restaurant. Numbers are under Locations.

Is your meat halal?

Yes. All our chicken and lamb is halal certified. Our prawns and fish are sustainably sourced, and we don't serve pork or beef.

Do you cater for allergies?

Every dish lists the 14 major allergens. Our kitchens use nuts, gluten and dairy, so there's a risk of cross-contact. Please tell us about any allergy when you book or order, and we'll talk you through the safest options.

Where do you deliver?

Each restaurant delivers to the postcodes around it, for example Belfast covers BT1 to BT9 and BT12 to BT15. Check your postcode in the Order section.

Can I bring my own wine?

Lisburn is bring your own with no corkage. Our other restaurants are fully licensed.

Do you have a kids menu?

Yes, in Lisburn, Bangor and Newry: mild chicken curry or chicken tikka with rice or chips, and a kulfi, for £7.95. Elsewhere we're happy to make half portions.

How do gift cards work?

They're emailed instantly, work in every restaurant and online, and are valid for 24 months. You can spend them over several visits.
